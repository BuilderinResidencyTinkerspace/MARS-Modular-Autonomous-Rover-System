# Week 1

**Goal this week:** 
 Starting to build a prototype of the base rover.

## What we did

- We first presented our project idea and discussed the basic requirements for building the base rover. We then cut a foam board into a *30 × 30 cm* size to form the initial base of the rover. We purchased the necessary components for the base rover, including the *ESP32-S3, ultrasonic sensors, motor driver, battery, buck converter, DC motors, and tyres. Since we could not get a suitable battery holder, we soldered the battery connections directly to obtain the required **12 V power supply* for the rover.


## Problems and blockers

- We could not get a suitable battery holder for the battery.
The battery had to be soldered directly to provide the required 12 V supply.

## Decisions

- Decided to use a *30 × 30 cm foam board* as the initial base of the rover.
Decided to solder the battery connections since a suitable battery holder was not available.

## Next week

- Start assembling the motors, wheels, motor driver and ESP32-S3 on the base.
Test the basic forward and backward movement of the rover.
Continue building the base prototype.

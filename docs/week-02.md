# Week 2

**Goal this week:**  Test the purchased components and learn about PCB designing and 3D printing.

## What we did

- This week, we focused mainly on testing the components that we had purchased for the base rover. We tested the *ultrasonic sensors, motor driver, DC motors, ESP32-S3, buck converter, battery, and other components* individually to make sure that they were working properly before integrating them into the rover.
- We also had a discussion with Kurian, the COO of TinkerSpace, who explained the basics of 3D printing and PCB designing. He explained how 3D printing works, including the importance of tolerances while designing and printing mechanical parts. He also introduced us to KiCad and explained the basic process of designing a PCB using it.
- Kurian also shared and explained some of the projects he had previously worked on. This helped us understand how different electronic and mechanical components can be combined to develop practical projects. The session gave us a better understanding of the design and manufacturing processes that we would need for our own project.

## Problems and blockers

- Some components required individual testing before being integrated into the rover.
We needed to understand the basics of PCB designing and 3D printing before starting our own designs.
Understanding the effect of 3D-printing tolerances on the final dimensions was also important for our future mechanical parts.

## Decisions

- We decided to test all the major components individually before integrating them into the rover.
We decided to use KiCad for PCB designing as we progress with the project.
We will consider 3D-printing tolerances while designing the mechanical components.

## Next week

- Continue working on the base structure of the rover.
Start designing the motor mounting structure.
Continue testing and integrating the electronic components.
Start working on the PCB design using KiCad.
Continue improving the mechanical parts based on the testing results.

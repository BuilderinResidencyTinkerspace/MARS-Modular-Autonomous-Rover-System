# Week 4

**Goal this week:** Make the MARS rover more autonomous by implementing obstacle avoidance and developing a system that allows the robot to move from one point to another.

## What we did

- This week, we worked on making the robot completely autonomous using ultrasonic sensors. Initially, we planned to place the ultrasonic sensors at the front, left and right sides of the robot so that it could detect obstacles from different directions and decide its movement accordingly. However, after testing the system, the robot did not behave as expected and the obstacle avoidance was not working reliably.
- We also wanted the robot to be able to move from one point to another without continuous manual control. For this, we planned to combine path following with obstacle avoidance, making the robot work similar to a line-following robot while also being able to detect and respond to obstacles.
- To help the robot follow a predefined path and understand the area in which it would operate, we measured the dimensions of the room and the path where the robot would be tested. These measurements were taken to understand the layout and plan how the robot could move from one location to another.
- At the same time, we worked on the wheel connection problem from the previous week and fixed the wheel issue, allowing the wheels to be properly attached and the rover to move correctly.
- After testing different ultrasonic sensor arrangements, we decided to place all three ultrasonic sensors at the front of the robot. This arrangement provided better obstacle detection in the direction of movement and was more suitable for our obstacle avoidance system.

## Problems and blockers

- The initial arrangement of ultrasonic sensors at the front, left and right did not provide the expected obstacle avoidance performance.
- Making the robot completely autonomous and allowing it to move from one point to another was more challenging than expected.
- The robot needed a reliable path-following method along with obstacle avoidance.

## Decisions

- We decided to place all three ultrasonic sensors at the front of the robot for better obstacle detection.'
- We decided to develop a system combining path following and obstacle avoidance.
- We measured the room and the required path to help plan the robot's movement between different points.
- The wheel connection problem was fixed, allowing us to continue with the rover development.

## Next week

- Continue developing the autonomous movement system.
- Work on the path-following system.
- Integrate path following with obstacle avoidance.
- Test the robot's ability to move from one point to another.
- Further test and improve the three-front-ultrasonic-sensor arrangement.

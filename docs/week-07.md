# Week 7

**Goal this week:** Improve the mechanical structure and reliability of the rover by replacing the tyres, transferring the temporary breadboard connections to a permanent perfboard setup, and integrating the components into the 3D-printed CAD design.

## What we did

- This week, we replaced the existing tyres with 65 mm Universal Hub tyres. The new tyres provided a better fit with the motors and improved the overall stability and movement of the rover.
- We started converting all the temporary breadboard connections into a permanent perfboard circuit. The components and wires were carefully arranged and soldered onto the perfboard to make the connections more reliable and suitable for the final rover.
- The process of soldering and arranging all the connections took a significant amount of time because we had to ensure that the wiring was correct and that there were no short circuits or loose connections.
- After completing the soldering, we checked the connections and tested the circuit to make sure the components were properly connected and functioning.
- We also 3D printed the CAD-designed parts required for the rover. After printing, we arranged the different components inside the designed structure according to the planned layout.
- The Arduino Uno, motor driver, battery and other components were positioned inside the 3D-printed structure. This helped us move from a temporary prototype setup towards a more organized and compact rover design.
- After arranging the components, we checked the available space and the positioning of the wiring to ensure that the components could be properly mounted without interfering with the movement of the rover.

## Problems and blockers

- Converting all the breadboard connections to a perfboard was time-consuming because every connection had to be soldered and checked individually.
- Proper wire arrangement was challenging because a large number of connections had to fit into a limited space.
- Extra care was required during soldering to avoid short circuits and incorrect connections.
- The 3D-printed parts required proper alignment and arrangement of the components inside the structure.
- The components had to be positioned carefully so that the wiring and mechanical parts did not interfere with each other.

## Decisions

- We decided to use 65 mm Universal Hub tyres for better compatibility and stability.
- We decided to replace the breadboard-based wiring with a permanent perfboard setup.
- We decided to solder and organize all the major circuit connections before finalizing the component placement.
- We decided to use the 3D-printed CAD structure to arrange and mount the rover components in a more compact and organized manner.
- We decided to focus on making the base rover mechanically and electrically stable before continuing with autonomous features.

## Next week

- Test the rover after transferring all connections to the perfboard.
- Integrate and test the ultrasonic sensors with the Arduino Uno.
- Start implementing autonomous obstacle avoidance.
- Test the rover's movement and obstacle detection with the new tyres and permanent wiring.
- Continue improving the overall mechanical and electrical arrangement of the rover.
